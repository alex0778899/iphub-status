How this is measured. A scheduled job on GitHub's infrastructure requests each public /health endpoint and records the result. The prober does not run on our servers, so it keeps reporting when our servers stop.

Honest limits. The job asks to run every 5 minutes, but the scheduler is best-effort and the measured interval is about 33 minutes — that figure is calculated from this page's own history, not assumed. An outage shorter than one interval can be missed entirely. Uptime here is "share of successful samples", not a contractual measurement. We publish no uptime SLA today; when we do, it will be backed by this history and by finer-grained monitoring than this.
